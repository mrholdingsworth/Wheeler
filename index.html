Wheeler

Sell the put, take the shares, sell the call. Basis is the number that matters. Every credit you bank comes off what you paid, every closed leg lands in the account to the penny, and the cash you have free sets the highest strike you can afford to sell next.

01 · Account
Deposits & withdrawals
Account value is the starting value plus deposits and closed P/L, to the penny. Shares count at what you paid for them, not at market — put a last price on a position to see where it stands. Fees per contract fill in the fee boxes as a starting guess; the number you type is the number recorded.
02 · Put Selector
Picks the mix of contracts with the most net credit that fits the capital, fees taken out per contract. Puts that miss your weekly minimum are left out. When expiries differ, each credit is scaled to a week so a two-week put has to earn twice as much to compete — with one expiry it is simply the largest total credit.

05 · Closed Positions
Account value over time — closed P/L and cash flows
Return on equity is total P/L over the most capital the position ever tied up — put collateral plus shares at cost. Annualized is simple, not compounded: that return times 365 over the days held, and anything shorter than a week counts as a week, because the cash cannot go back to work before the next Friday.
06 · Data Management
Everything lives in this browser's localStorage. Clearing site data wipes it, and nothing leaves this machine.
